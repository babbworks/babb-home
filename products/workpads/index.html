§ 01What it doessix things, in order of how often they matter
01

Structured forms that don’t fight you.

Required where it must be, optional where it should be. Built per Place — a feed-lot is not a bank branch.

tap targets ≥ 44px · gloves-on tested
02

Photos attached to fields, not to records.

A photo lives on the entry it documents — not in a separate bucket. The reviewer sees them together every time.

EXIF · timestamp · GPS optional
03

Audio on every entry.

Tap-and-hold to record. Transcribed when there’s a signal. Useful for the half-sentence you couldn’t type fast enough.

on-device transcription · whisper-class
04

Offline-first sync.

Works when it can. Syncs when it can’t. Conflict resolution that favors the person who was there.

CRDT · queue length unlimited
05

CSV, JSON, PDF. The record you make in Workpads is yours, on disk, readable in any tool. We do not hold your data hostage.

CSV · JSON · PDF · webhook

Farming · Sioux Falls feed-lot

Pen rotation, on the rail.

Cal walks 48 pens before dawn. Three required fields, one optional photo, one audio quip on the entries he’s not sure about. The record prints on a label roll on the corner of his desk before the morning crew arrives.

Manufacturing · Hamilton steel mill

Shift handoff in five questions.

The third shift gives way to the morning shift. Five required questions, three weekly photos, two attached audio clips for “what to watch for.” Eight months in, average handoff time is down 41% and the foreman has stopped printing.

Banking · Hudson community bank

Branch open, branch close.
