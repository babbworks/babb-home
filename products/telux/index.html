Telux.

System for Group Exchange. A proposed operating system layer that unifies group identity, power management, and transactional security into a single coherent primitive. No existing OS combines all three. Telux does.

§ 01Core conceptsnewgroup · Islands · Outstack
01

newgroup.

An evolution of the Linux group primitive. A newgroup can contain human users, system services, commercial APIs, AI models, and IoT-connected physical systems. Dynamic, permanent or temporary.

humans · agents · services · IoT
02

Islands.

Sovereign containers. Every group belongs to an Island. Islands are the primary security boundary. Each must declare who or what is Sovereign over it. Sovereignty is hierarchical and grantable.

sovereignty · security boundary
03

Three-layer architecture.

Visible (group chat, NL query, exchange records), Submerged (daemon, sovereignty enforcer, power governor), Bedrock (LSM module, immutable audit, TPM/HSM keys, power gates).

visible · submerged · bedrock
04

Power as security.

Islands receive electricity budgets. Exceeding that budget is a security event. At exec() time, Outstack checks whether the power budget permits the process. In EMERGENCY mode, only CRITICAL-class processes run.

power gating · physics-based security
05

Transactional exchange.

Every exchange between entities in a group is recorded before it completes. Hashed, signed, distributed to the appropriate visibility tier. The write-ahead log is the bedrock of trust.

write-ahead · signed · auditable
06

Non-human identity.

AI models, IoT sensors, and automated services get first-class group membership with capabilities, power budgets, and auditable exchange records. W3C Decentralized Identifiers for all non-human members.

DIDs · AI · IoT · first-class
