§ 01The buildingsvon Neumann extended with Gate, Office, Actors
01

Intake.

Information arrives at a defined entry point. Verified, classified, normalized, routed. Nothing enters the compound unexamined.

receive · verify · classify · route
02

Store.

Named, addressed repositories using Row × Level × Bin coordinates. Immutable once written. Lifecycle stages: Raw → Staged → Integrated.

addressed · immutable · three levels
03

Mill.

The transformation engine. Group, reorder, title, tag, annotate, concatenate. Ingress → operation → egress. Interactive or automated via Barrels.

transform · combine · lens pipeline
04

Gate.

Nothing leaves without authorization. Every chunk must pass Gate before Dispatch. Five decisions: approve, reject, hold, reroute, archive. The Right to Assembly.

mandatory · 5 decisions · veto
05

Dispatch.

Five delivery channels: download, clipboard, filesystem, cross-compound, WebWarrior bridge. Only Gate-approved chunks can be dispatched. Vault archives on delivery.

5 channels · gate-verified · archived
06

Office.

The policy authority. Issues capability tokens, maintains actor registry, enforces rules, records audit logs, vetoes unauthorized actions. The constitutional layer.

tokens · registry · audit · veto
