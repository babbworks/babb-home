SIMBA.

A standard for long-lived, interoperable business systems. Part of the Babb family of protocols and specifications alongside BASICS, BitPads, and the Works compound standard. Standards outlast software — a protocol specification can be reimplemented by anyone, on any platform, at any time.

§ 01What it iscentury-capable systems
01

Normative standard.

Like BASICS, SIMBA defines testable obligations rather than suggested practices. Conforming implementations must satisfy the specification. Verifiable, not aspirational.

testable · normative · verifiable
02

Century-capable.

Designed to operate across major economic and technological shifts. Not just the current product cycle. The standard persists even when the software that implements it is replaced.

decades · not quarters
03

Part of the family.

Sits alongside BASICS (durability), BitPads (binary communication), BitLedger (financial transmission), and Works (information compound) in the broader Babb architecture.
