§ 01About Babb Works

Babb is a media outfit and a product company. We report from the working world — feed lots, steel mills, branch offices, job sites — and we build tools for the people we cover.

The same field that shapes our coverage shapes our software and hardware. We are not a technology company that went looking for problems. We are reporters and builders who found that the tools their subjects needed did not exist.
