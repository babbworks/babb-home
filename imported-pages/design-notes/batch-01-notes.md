## Themes I'm picking up

1. **Stone, tessellation, geometry.** Triangles, mosaics, icosahedra, infinity spirals — recurring motif across products and the @ monument. There's a "carved artifact" quality to the brand.
2. **Sub-brand family.** Babb (parent) → Babb Products, Babb Works, Workpads / workpads.me, Bitpads, .tel. Two visual registers coexist: clean catalog (Products) and playful hand-drawn (Works / Workpads).
3. **Hand-drawn vs. precise.** The marker-style "babb" / "@workpads.me" wordmarks and the mascot live opposite the surgical product photography and the engineered triangle truss. The homepage probably wants to hold both.
4. **Mascot-led warmth.** The "Hi." green character is a strong, ownable greeting device — could anchor the top of the page.
5. **Mythic/folk-tech imagery.** Brand-as-monument shots (Mars .tel, desert @) suggest comfort with weird, oversized, slightly absurd hero imagery rather than generic SaaS gradients.
6. **Punctuation & symbols as system.** # = * ~ ○ / on the t-shirt, the @ sculpture, the .tel sphere — punctuation is doing identity work.
7. **Heritage / labor / time.** Punch clock, steel mill, mancala, NASA print — gravitas, things that endure, "work" with a capital W.
8. **Atmospheric counter-note.** The Turner painting and the relief map are the quiet, foggy, contemplative side — useful as section breaks or full-bleed pauses.
