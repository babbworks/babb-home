§ 04One product at a time Read after scan
01 — Workpads

A field notebook that survives the field.

Built with the people we cover. Workpads pairs structured forms with audio, photo and signoff so a single pass through a site — a pen, a furnace, a branch lobby — produces a complete record. Syncs when it can; works when it can’t.

iOS · Android · Web
Offline-first sync
Audio + photo attached to fields
Open export (CSV, JSON, PDF)
Workpads →Field reports →
02 — Bitpads

One small piece of hardware. Weeks of battery.

An e-ink companion for Workpads. Sits in a pocket or on a clipboard. Reads tags, captures counts, prints to a small label run. Designed for places where a phone is the wrong tool — gloves on, cold weather, wet hands.

Rev C · firmware 0.4
Sub-GHz radio + BLE
~30 day idle
USB-C · field replaceable cell
Bitpads →Changelog →
03 — .tel

An identity layer for people who do work in the world.

.tel routes the noise away from the people who need to reach you, and toward the ones who actually do. A small protocol with a long view. Plants like infrastructure. Built to outlast a platform cycle.

Alpha · invite only
Open spec
Email · SMS · channels
Self-hostable nodes
