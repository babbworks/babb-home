    Treatment

    A working integrated steel mill on Hamilton harbour. We're following it through one full production week as it navigates decarbonization, automation, and a town that grew up around it.

    Standing stories aren’t pieces — they’re places we keep coming back to. This page accumulates: four canonical angles, a cast of people, a growing B-roll archive, field notes from our editors on the ground, and dailies as we ship them. The bylaws of how we work here are the same as the mill’s: show up, count what you see, write it down honestly.
