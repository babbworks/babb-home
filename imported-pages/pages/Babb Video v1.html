Director’s note

We came back three times before we could shoot. The first visit was a scout; the second was a write-around when the maintenance window didn’t hold; the third was the shoot, which started at three in the morning and ended when the second-shift foreman walked in for the morning brief. You don’t set up around the work; you set up inside it and you turn the camera on when the work doesn’t notice you.

The piece is cut to the cadence of a heat — charge, blow, tap, cast — rather than to a narrative arc. The third-shift quiet is the subject; the foreman is the voice. If you watch it with the lights down, the score is almost entirely the ambient field recording from D.L.’s mics at 06:14, which is the same recording that loops on the Place page.

— Editor A, 2026-05-11
