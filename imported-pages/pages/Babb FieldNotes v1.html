May 12Day 042026-05-12 · Tuesday
Final pour day. Three notes from the floor — one about the cover image, two anchored to The Floor. Crew breaks down at noon.
N 1417:42 ET · on site anchored to The Floor · dawn strip
The third shift was unexpectedly the most photogenic — reconsider the cover image. Talk to the shoot crew about returning at 05:00 local next pass. The dawn light hits the bays at an angle nothing else does, and the floor is quieter so the people fill the frame.
— Editor A permalinkin Dailies
N 1314:08 ET anchored to The Floor · Numbers
Find a chart for cost-per-ton that doesn’t read like a spreadsheet. Maybe a paper-ledger style with handwritten weeks. The version Marisol showed us in the office at lunch had columns drawn in pencil and was more legible than anything we’ve produced.
— Editor A permalinkdispatch graf 9
N 1209:30 ET anchored to Audio · ambient
The corridor outside the pulpit gives a cleaner ambient signal than the pulpit itself; less HVAC hum, more cast hum bleeding through the door. Recording the next loop from there. Replace the AMB 01 mix on the Place page after the next pass.
— D.L., sound permalinkB-Roll · audio
May 11Day 032026-05-11 · Monday
Tony R.’s long interview day. Four notes — including the one that changed how we treat ladle metallurgy on every page going forward.
N 1111:42 ET anchored to Glossary · on every page
“Ladle metallurgy” deserves its own popup on hover wherever it appears. Move to glossary, not body. Same for tap-to-tap, EAF, DRI, scope-3. The Angle is already too jargon-heavy; the prose shouldn’t apologize for being specific but it should let the reader catch up.
— Editor A permalinkglossary
N 1014:42 ET anchored to Audio · Tony R. · 14:42
“The console keeps the number. We keep everything else.” That’s the line. Lead the Article pull-quote with this, not with the floor-doesn’t-behave one. The floor-doesn’t-behave line is more T-shirt; this one is more newspaper.
— Editor A permalinkdispatch
N 0916:18 ET general · Building
Cross-link this Place to Building. The mill is also a civil-scale construction story whenever they re-line the furnace. We’ve been treating it as Manufacturing-only and missing the 2028 rebuild as an angle in itself. Add Building as a secondary World; consider whether The Future deserves to split into two.
— Editor B permalinknoted in editor’s notes
N 0817:08 ET anchored to The Future
Marisol and Tony R. have different answers about the 2028 rebuild cycle. The Angle currently presents the question without choosing; don’t resolve it yet. We’ll come back at the 2027 shutdown brief and again at the start of 2028 work.
— Editor A permalink
May 10Day 022026-05-10 · Sunday
Town walk after second shift. Three notes — one on photographs vs. textures, two on East-end framing.
N 0720:42 ET anchored to The Town
Walk after the second shift — the route from gate 4 to Tony’s street is its own beat. Bar opposite gate 4, the walkway over the train yard, the school across the road, the harbour bridge view. Build the Town angle around this route. A series, not a single hero photo.
— Editor A permalinkB-Roll · town
N 0622:14 ET general · design system
Until B.K. signs off on photo selects, the Worlds plates and the Place hero stay as CSS textures. Be honest about what is rendering and what was captured. Don’t paste in stock or AI-generated stand-ins. Editor’s note draft in progress — should publish before the Place page goes live.
— Editor B permalinknotes archive
N 0508:08 ET anchored to The Town
The school across the road is the cleanest visual argument for how the mill and the town interleave. Drop-off at 08:00 ET overlaps with first-shift arrival; pickup at 15:00 overlaps with the first-to-second handoff. Frame at least two photographs on this clock.
— Editor A permalink
May 09Day 012026-05-09 · Saturday
Arrival day. Two notes setting the framework for the week ahead, including the decision to lead the page with the dawn photograph.
N 0406:14 ET · first frame anchored to The Floor · dawn strip
Dawn light through the high windows at 06:14 local. This is the cover image. Decision made standing in the pulpit at 05:58. Next visit should arrive at 03:00 to be standing there by 04:00 with the cameras set. Mark on the schedule.
— Editor A permalinkthe Place page lead
N 0310:08 ET anchored to The Owners · timeline
The headcount-over-a-century chart on The Owners reads better in absolute numbers, not percentages. People know what 4,200 people means. They don’t know what 38% of peak workforce means. Switch the Y-axis before publish.
— Editor B permalink
Apr 25Pre-trip2026-04-25 · standing story opened
Notes from the scoping calls, ten days before crew arrived. The bylaws that shaped the week.
N 02scoping call · 11:42 ET general · posture
Frame the standing story around the mill’s own bylaws: show up, count what you see, write it down honestly. Don’t bring an arc to the floor. The floor has its own. The Dailies are the through-line; the Angles are the slow read; the dispatch is the cut.
— Editor B permalinkposture / about
N 01scoping call · 14:08 ET general · Place setup
Four canonical Angles for v1 of this Place: The Floor, The Owners, The Town, The Future. Hold the line at four until we’ve been back twice. Add more only when the material demands it; resist the urge to chase the day’s shiny thing.
— Editor A permalink
